Questions

Is the diagnosis really free?

Yes. You answer a few questions and see the result on the screen right away. No sign-up, no email, no password.

Are you Meta or Instagram support?

No. This is an independent service, not affiliated with Meta. We can't see your account and can't change Meta's decisions. The official appeal is always made by you, in the app.

Can you get my account back?

No one outside Meta can restore an account, and anyone who promises it for money is a red flag. What we do is explain what happened, the deadline and how to appeal correctly.

What is in the $19 personal report?

A PDF made from your screenshots: which restriction it is and which Meta rule it refers to, deadlines, a step-by-step plan, what not to do, an appeal text for your case and similar real cases. Delivered by email within 24 hours.

What is the difference between suspended and disabled?

Suspended means you still have time to appeal, usually 180 days. Disabled with no appeal button and no deadline usually means the decision is final in the app.
