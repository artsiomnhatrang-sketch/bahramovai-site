Instagram appeal form: where it is and what to write

By Artsiom Bahram, independent consultant on Instagram and Facebook account restrictions · Updated November 9, 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer: For a suspended or disabled account, the appeal form is inside the app: log into that account and tap Disagree with decision or Request review. For a removed post, open Account Status and appeal from there. Write 2-3 calm, factual sentences. A link to an appeal form sent to you in DMs or by a stranger is a scam.

Where is the appeal form for a suspended or disabled account?

In 2026 the main appeal path is in the app, not on a website. Log into the account that was suspended or disabled, and the screen itself shows Disagree with decision, Appeal or Request review. Tapping it starts the form: a code, sometimes a video selfie or an ID, and sometimes a text field.

If you don't see any of these buttons and no deadline, the appeal was most likely already reviewed, the time ran out, or the decision came without an appeal option. There is no hidden form that reopens it.

Where do you appeal a removed post or a warning?

  1. Open your profile → menu → Settings and activity → Account Status.
  2. Open Removed content (or the recommendations or features section, depending on the problem).
  3. Tap the post → Disagree with decision and briefly explain the context: humor, news, education, your own photo.

If the appeal is rejected and the answer includes a reference number for the Oversight Board, you can submit the case there within the stated time, usually 15 days.

What about forms in the Help Center?

The Help Center has its own forms for specific cases: a hacked account (instagram.com/hacked), reporting impersonation, copyright counter-notices. Forms for disabled accounts have changed many times and don't open for everyone. If the Help Center itself offers you one, fill it in once, with exactly the same name, email and username as on the account. It doesn't replace the appeal in the app.

What should you write in an appeal?

Short and factual. The reviewer needs to know whose account it is, what it's used for and why you think the decision is wrong. Example:

Hello. This is my personal account @[username], which I use for [my small bakery / photos of my family]. I believe it was suspended by mistake: I post my own content and don't use automation or growth services. I have completed the identity check. Please review the decision. Thank you.

Adapt it to the truth of your case. If you did use a growth service or automation, say that you have turned it off and removed its access.

What should you not write?

  • Threats, insults or "I'll sue" - they don't speed anything up.
  • Long stories and copied legal texts - one paragraph is enough.
  • Anything untrue. The appeal is a statement to Meta; a false one makes things worse.
  • Your password or codes - no real form asks for them in a text field.

How do you spot a fake appeal form?

Scammers know that people with a disabled account search for an "appeal form". Signs of a fake:

  • It came to you in a DM, a comment or an email from a non-Meta address. Real emails come from @mail.instagram.com or @facebookmail.com and are listed in Accounts Center → Password and security → Recent emails.
  • It threatens to delete your account within hours.
  • It asks for your password, a login code or a payment.

From practice: DMs closed for "fraud" - a human review admitted the mistake

Sending messages was blocked for a month for "Fraud and deception", and the bio link couldn't be changed. The cause turned out to be direct messenger links in DMs and the bio, plus many automated messages. The owner removed them and tapped Request review once. A human reviewer checked it and admitted the mistake. A "fraud" restriction is often a false positive: remove whatever looks like a scam first, then appeal once.

FAQ

Where is the Instagram appeal form?

For a suspended or disabled account it's in the app: log into that account and tap Disagree with decision or Request review. For removed posts, appeal from Account Status.

Is there an Instagram appeal form for disabled accounts on the web?

The Help Center has forms for specific cases such as hacked accounts, but forms for disabled accounts change often and don't open for everyone. They don't replace the appeal in the app.

What should I write in an Instagram appeal?

Two or three calm, true sentences: whose account it is, what it's used for and why you believe the decision is a mistake. Mention that you completed the identity check.

How many times can I submit an Instagram appeal?

Submit it once and complete every check. Repeated appeals don't help, and after a rejection the app usually stops offering another review.

How do I know an Instagram appeal form is fake?

If it came in a DM, a comment or from a non-Meta email, threatens deletion within hours, or asks for your password, a code or a payment, it's a scam.
