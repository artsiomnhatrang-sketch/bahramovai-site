Facebook account disabled: what to do next

By Artsiom Bahram, independent consultant on Instagram and Facebook account restrictions · Updated November 9, 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer: If Facebook shows Disagree with decision, Request review or a number of days left, the decision isn't final: appeal once in the app or on facebook.com and complete the check (code, video selfie, ID). If there is no button and no deadline, it's usually final. If you were hacked, go to facebook.com/hacked instead. Check your Instagram separately: accounts in the same Accounts Center are often restricted together.

How do you appeal a disabled Facebook account?

  1. Log in on the phone or computer you usually use, VPN off.
  2. Tap Disagree with decision or Request review.
  3. Complete the code, video selfie or ID upload. Use an ID with your name, date of birth and photo; the name should match the profile.
  4. If there is a text field, write 2-3 factual sentences about whose account it is and why you believe it's a mistake. Example of an appeal text (the same approach works for Facebook).
  5. Wait for the answer in the app and by email from @facebookmail.com. Don't appeal again while it's under review.

Will my Instagram be affected?

It can be. Accounts in one Accounts Center, on one phone or run the same way are often restricted together, in a chain. Open Account Status on each Instagram account and check it separately. Deleting Facebook doesn't erase the links: Meta keeps them.

Facebook ads restricted: is that the same thing?

No. "Your account is restricted from advertising" leaves your profile and Messenger working, but you can't run or boost ads, including on Instagram. Open Account Quality (business.facebook.com/accountquality), find the restriction and tap Request review; check for unpaid ad balances, which block advertising on their own.

From practice: ads restricted because of someone else's ad account

A personal Facebook got "restricted from advertising" for links to untrustworthy accounts. The cause: the person had once been an Advertiser in someone else's ad account, which was disabled with unpaid debt. The owners couldn't be reached, so the role couldn't be removed. The profile itself was fine - only ads were restricted, and deleting Facebook wouldn't have cleared the debt or the links. Don't accept ad account roles from people you don't trust, and leave roles when a project ends.

What if the decision is final?

  • EU residents can take the decision to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body under the Digital Services Act, for example Appeals Centre Europe.
  • Meta Verified on a linked account (an Instagram in the same Accounts Center) lets you ask support about it, but they often can't act on a different account.
  • Pages and ad accounts stay manageable if they have a second admin. If they don't, add a trusted second admin on your other assets now, before anything else happens.

Be careful with "recovery" offers in comments and DMs: no one outside Meta can restore an account, and anyone asking for your password or an upfront payment is a red flag.

FAQ

Can I get my disabled Facebook account back?

If Facebook still shows Disagree with decision, Request review or days left, appeal once and complete the check. With no button and no deadline, the decision is usually final.

How long does Facebook take to review a disabled account?

From minutes after an identity check to a few weeks. The answer comes in the app and by email from @facebookmail.com.

Will my Instagram be disabled if Facebook disabled my account?

Not always, but accounts in the same Accounts Center are often restricted together. Check Account Status on each Instagram account separately.

What is the difference between a disabled account and restricted ads?

A disabled account can't be used at all. An advertising restriction blocks running and boosting ads, including on Instagram; the profile and Messenger keep working. Ads are appealed in Account Quality.

What should I do if my Facebook was hacked?

Go to facebook.com/hacked right away, use the revert link in the security email if there is one, then set a new password and two-factor authentication.
