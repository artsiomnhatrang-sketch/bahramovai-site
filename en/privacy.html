Privacy and refunds

Updated November 9, 2026

The free diagnosis

The diagnosis has no forms: we don't ask for your name, email, username or password. The site uses Yandex Metrica, an analytics service, to count visits and see which pages and answers are useful. It records anonymous page activity (clicks, scrolling, session replay) and events such as "diagnosis finished". We use this only to improve the site and don't sell it.

The personal report

Payment is handled by Gumroad; we don't see your card details. To make the report, you send us screenshots of what your app shows. We use them only to prepare your report and don't share them. Please hide anything you don't want us to see, such as other people's messages. We never ask for your password or login codes - if anyone does in our name, it's not us.

Refunds

The report is delivered by email within 24 hours after you send your screenshots. If it doesn't arrive within 24 hours, email us at the address in your Gumroad receipt and you get a full refund. The report explains your situation and how to appeal; the decision on any account is made only by Meta, so the outcome of an appeal is not a reason for a refund.
