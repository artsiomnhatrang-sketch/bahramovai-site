Instagram account disabled: reasons, deadlines and real chances

By Artsiom Bahram, independent consultant on Instagram and Facebook account restrictions · Updated November 9, 2026 · 7 min read

Short answer: Instagram disables accounts for repeated violations, one very serious violation, signs of fake or linked accounts, or when it can't confirm who owns the account. If you still see an Appeal button or days left (usually 180), the decision isn't final and you can appeal in the app. With no button and no deadline, it usually is final.

Why does Instagram disable accounts?

Meta rarely tells you the full reason, but the notice usually names a policy. The most common ones I see:

  • Accumulated warnings. Each removed post can count as a warning (a "strike"). One doesn't block an account; many in a row do. They stop counting after about a year.
  • One very serious violation. For the most severe categories, such as child safety, Meta can disable an account after a single violation.
  • Account integrity and authentic identity. Several accounts run the same way by one person, an account that pretends to be someone else, or a new account created after a block.
  • Spam and inauthentic behavior. Growth services, mass follows and DMs, auto-replies that send links, bought followers.
  • Fraud and scams. Often a false positive: "free" offers plus a direct link to a messenger or bot in the bio look like a known scam pattern.
  • Age and identity checks not passed. If Instagram can't confirm the owner is at least 13 or who owns the account, it can suspend it until you do.

How long do you have to appeal?

The screen shows how many days are left, most often 180 from the date of the decision. Don't leave it for later: if the time runs out without an appeal, the account is disabled permanently. If your appeal is rejected, the screen usually stops offering another review.

What are the real chances?

It depends on why the account was disabled. What I see in practice:

  • Identity and security checks are often resolved quickly once you complete the video selfie or code from your usual phone.
  • "Fraud" and spam flags on a normal business account are often mistakes and can be reversed by a human review, once the trigger (links, automation) is removed.
  • Networks of linked accounts are the hardest: while the links remain, an appeal can lift the block only for a while.
  • Final decisions with no button rarely change. Anyone who promises otherwise for money is a red flag.

From practice: five linked accounts disabled in a chain

One owner had five accounts with identical posts, the same chatbots, all linked to one Facebook. Over two months they were disabled one after another; the main one was blocked seven times. Appeals, selfies and a passport brought it back - and a few days later it was blocked again. Two accounts were saved, three were lost. Meta blocks not for one post but for links between accounts: while identical content, shared bots and one Facebook remain, an appeal lifts the block only for a while.

Is my phone blocked too?

Usually not. If another account of yours works fine on the same phone, the phone isn't blocked. Resetting the phone doesn't help either: Meta links accounts by many signals (devices, contacts, content, bots, Accounts Center), so a reset doesn't break the link.

Can you just open a new account?

Instagram's Terms of Use don't allow a new account after one was disabled for a violation without Instagram's permission. In practice, a new account linked to the old one by phone, contacts, content or Accounts Center is usually disabled as circumvention, and it can drag your other accounts with it.

FAQ

Why did Instagram disable my account without a reason?

The notice usually names a policy, such as account integrity, spam or fraud. Common triggers are accumulated warnings, growth or automation services, several accounts run the same way, and identity checks that were not passed.

How long does a disabled Instagram account stay disabled?

If you still see an appeal button or days left, it's not final until the deadline, usually 180 days. With no button and no deadline, the decision is usually permanent in the app.

What is the difference between suspended and disabled on Instagram?

Suspended means you still have time to disagree, usually 180 days. Disabled with no appeal button and no deadline usually means the appeal was reviewed, the time ran out, or there was no appeal option.

Is my phone banned if Instagram disabled my account?

Usually not. If another account works on the same phone, the phone isn't blocked. Resetting the phone doesn't help: Meta links accounts by many signals, not just the device.

Can I create a new Instagram account after mine was disabled?

Instagram's Terms of Use don't allow it without Instagram's permission, and a new account linked to the old one is usually disabled as circumvention.
