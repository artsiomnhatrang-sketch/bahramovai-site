Instagram account suspended: how to appeal before the deadline

By Artsiom Bahram, independent consultant on Instagram and Facebook account restrictions · Updated November 9, 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer: A suspended account is hidden and you can't use it, but it's not final: you usually have 180 days to disagree. Log in, tap Disagree with decision, complete the code or video selfie and wait for the answer in the app and by email. If you don't appeal in time, or the appeal is rejected, the account is disabled permanently.

What does "We suspended your account" mean?

Nobody can see your profile, and you can't post, message or browse. The screen names the policy (for example "Fraud and scams" or "Account integrity") and shows how many days you have to disagree. Until that time runs out, the decision isn't final.

A suspension often means Instagram wants to check who owns the account, not that it has already decided you broke the rules for good.

How do you appeal a suspension, step by step?

  1. Open the Instagram app on the phone you usually use, on your home network, VPN off.
  2. Log into the suspended account and tap Disagree with decision (or Appeal).
  3. Complete everything you're asked for: a captcha, a code from email or SMS, a video selfie (turn your head slowly, good light, no glasses or hat), sometimes an ID.
  4. If there is a text field, write 2-3 calm, factual sentences. Example of what to write.
  5. Wait. The answer comes in the app and by email; check Spam too.

From practice: suspended for "fraud", resolved by a selfie check

An old business account got the "We suspended your account" screen for "Fraud and scams", with 180 days to appeal. The owner tapped Appeal right away and completed the captcha, email code and video selfie. Seven minutes later an email arrived: "You can use your account again". A month later the same thing happened, and a selfie restored it again the same morning. A suspension often means an identity check, not a final verdict: complete the check before writing long letters. This one was fast; other reviews take weeks, and the outcome is always Meta's decision.

What happens after you appeal?

  • "You can use your account again" - the account is back. In my clients' cases it helped to keep things calm for about a week: no new phone, no VPN, no country change, so the check doesn't start over. Turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Waiting - the review can take from a day to a few weeks. Don't send the appeal again.
  • "We disabled your account" with no further button - the appeal was rejected and the decision is usually final. See what you can still do.

Why do suspensions happen?

  • A new phone, a VPN or a login from another country, followed by a spike in activity.
  • Growth, auto-follow and mass-DM services, or auto-replies that send links to strangers.
  • "Free" offers plus a direct link to a messenger or bot in the bio: it matches a known scam pattern.
  • Several accounts on one phone or in one Accounts Center with the same content.
  • A team or agency logging into one account from many devices and countries.

Which mistakes make a suspension permanent?

  • Missing the deadline. No appeal in time means permanent disabling.
  • A new account in the meantime. It counts as circumventing enforcement and can get the new and the linked accounts disabled.
  • Many appeals in a row. One honest appeal with a completed check works better.
  • Leaving the trigger in place. If automation or a risky link stays, the account can be suspended again soon after it's restored. Getting blocked again doesn't mean your phone is blocked; more often the account trips over the same cause.

FAQ

How long does an Instagram suspension last?

Until you appeal and Meta decides. You usually have 180 days to disagree; if you don't, the account is disabled permanently.

How long does Instagram take to respond to a suspension appeal?

From a few minutes after a video selfie to a few weeks. The answer comes in the app and by email, including the Spam folder.

Why was my Instagram account suspended for no reason?

Common triggers are a new phone or VPN, growth or mass-DM services, auto-replies with links, several linked accounts and team logins from many devices. Often it's an identity check rather than a final verdict.

Can I appeal an Instagram suspension more than once?

Send one appeal and complete every check. Repeated appeals don't speed things up, and after a rejection the screen usually stops offering another review.

What should I do after my suspended account is restored?

Keep things calm for about a week: no new phone, no VPN, no country change. Turn on two-factor authentication and remove whatever caused it, such as automation or risky links.
